PromptGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

PromptGo is an online AI prompt library. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the PromptGo Android application.

1. Account and information

PromptGo does not require an in-app user account. Favorites, language, theme and accent-color preferences are stored locally on your device. PromptGo does not request precise location, contacts, photos or microphone access for ordinary browsing.

2. Firebase and notifications

PromptGo uses Google Firebase Realtime Database to retrieve prompt posts, creator information and app-update information. If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a device registration token to deliver new-post notifications. Firebase may process technical connection, delivery and diagnostic information needed to operate these services. See Firebase privacy and security and Google Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

Information is used to display online content, check whether an update is required, remember preferences on your device and deliver notifications when enabled. PromptGo does not currently include in-app advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

4. Sharing and external links

PromptGo does not sell personal information. Firebase is a service provider used to operate the app. If you open an email, WhatsApp, website or social-media link, the selected third-party service handles your information under its own privacy policy. Prompt images may be hosted on external websites and are retrieved from those websites when displayed.

5. Retention and deletion

Local preferences and favorites remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Notification data is handled by Firebase according to its service operation and retention rules. PromptGo does not maintain a user account. For questions or requests concerning information handled by the developer, contact the email below.

6. Security

PromptGo uses HTTPS connections for supported online services. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to database and administrative functions is restricted through the project's security configuration.

7. Children

PromptGo is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact us.

8. Your choices

You can disable notifications in Android settings, clear locally stored app data, uninstall the app, or choose not to open external contact and social-media links.

9. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page.
